FAQ

Common refrigeration questions

Do you repair fridges that are not cooling?

Yes. Contact us with the fridge symptoms and your area. We can assess the request and advise on the appropriate repair.

Do you repair ice makers?

Yes. We handle ice maker faults including units that stop producing ice, produce too slowly or have cooling-related problems.

Do you refill refrigeration gas?

Yes. Gas/ref refrigerant service is available. Proper diagnosis is important because low gas can be caused by a leak or another underlying fault.

Do you repair coldrooms?

Yes. Contact us with the coldroom type, temperature problem and location so we can assess the service requirement.

Which areas do you cover?

We serve Cape Town and the wider Western Cape. Send your suburb when requesting a service and we can confirm availability.
